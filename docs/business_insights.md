# Business Insights Guide

Use the following insight framing when presenting the dashboard.

## Executive Overview

The executive overview shows total revenue, units sold, average selling price, and sales trend over time. This helps stakeholders understand whether the business is growing, stable, or declining.

## Product & Brand Performance

Top product and brand analysis identifies which SKUs and brands contribute the most revenue and sales volume. This can support assortment planning and product prioritization.

## Channel & Region Performance

Channel and region analysis shows where sales are strongest. This helps identify whether retail, discount, or e-commerce channels are driving growth in each region.

## Promotion Impact

Promotion analysis compares promoted and non-promoted records. If promoted records show higher units sold but lower average price, stakeholders can evaluate whether promotions are driving volume at the cost of price.

## Inventory & Delivery Performance

Inventory and delivery analysis highlights operational patterns that may influence sales. Longer delivery days or low stock availability can affect sales performance and should be monitored.

## Demand Forecasting

Demand forecasting predicts `units_sold` using historical product, price, promotion, stock, date, channel, and region features. This supports demand planning and inventory decision-making.
