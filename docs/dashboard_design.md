# Dashboard Design

## Dashboard Title

**FMCG Sales Demand Forecasting & Performance Analytics**

## Page 1 — Executive Sales Overview

Purpose: show overall business performance.

Visuals:

- Total Revenue — KPI card
- Total Units Sold — KPI card
- Average Selling Price — KPI card
- Unique SKUs — KPI card
- Unique Brands — KPI card
- Monthly Revenue Trend — Line chart

## Page 2 — Product & Brand Performance

Purpose: identify the largest product and brand contributors.

Visuals:

- Top SKUs by Revenue — Bar chart
- Top Brands by Revenue — Bar chart
- Category Revenue Share — Bar or pie chart
- Top Pack Types by Revenue — Bar chart

## Page 3 — Channel & Region Performance

Purpose: compare sales performance across channels and regions.

Visuals:

- Revenue by Channel — Bar chart
- Units Sold by Channel — Bar chart
- Revenue by Region — Bar chart
- Channel x Region Revenue Matrix — Table or heatmap

## Page 4 — Promotion Impact

Purpose: compare promoted and non-promoted sales.

Visuals:

- Revenue by Promotion Status — Bar chart
- Units Sold by Promotion Status — Bar chart
- Average Price by Promotion Status — Bar chart
- Promotion Share of Revenue — Pie chart

## Page 5 — Inventory & Delivery Performance

Purpose: understand supply and operational signals.

Visuals:

- Average Delivery Days by Region — Bar chart
- Stock Availability by Category — Bar chart
- Delivered Quantity vs Units Sold — Scatter plot
- Data Quality Issue Count — Table

## Page 6 — Demand Forecasting Results

Purpose: show ML output and business use.

Visuals:

- Actual vs Predicted Units Sold — Scatter plot
- Forecast Error by Category — Bar chart
- Top SKUs by Predicted Demand — Bar chart
- Prediction Detail Table — Table
